# Client FAQ — PWA Starter Template

## 1. What is a PWA?

A Progressive Web App is a website that works like a native app. Users can install it on their home screen, use it offline, and get an app-like experience — all from the browser.

## 2. Do I need to be in the App Store?

No. PWAs install directly from the browser. No Apple/Google approval needed.

## 3. Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. PWAs work on all modern browsers — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.

## 4. Can my customers use it offline?

Yes. The service worker caches the app so it loads and works without an internet connection.

## 5. Will it slow down my website?

No. The PWA is a separate installable app. It doesn't affect your existing site.

## 6. Can I send push notifications?

Yes on Android. Safari (iOS) is adding support — it's in beta as of 2026. Works on desktop Chrome and Firefox today.

## 7. How much does it cost?

The template starts at $500. Custom features, branding, and content are quoted per project. See the Pricing Sheet.

## 8. How long until it's live?

Basic setup: 1–3 business days. Full customization with content: 1–2 weeks.

## 9. Can I update it after launch?

Yes. Updates deploy instantly — no App Store resubmission. Your customers always get the latest version.

## 10. What if I already have a website?

The PWA can complement your existing site or replace it. It's a standalone app that you link to from your current site.
