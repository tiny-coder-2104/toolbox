# PWA Pitch Deck — 5-Slide Outline

Use this to sell the PWA template to clients. Customize for each pitch.

---

## Slide 1: The Problem

**"Your customers need your business on their phone — but native apps are expensive."**

- Native app development: $15K–$50K+
- App Store approval process: weeks to months
- Maintenance burden: every OS update can break things
- Most small businesses can't justify the cost

---

## Slide 2: The Solution

**"A Progressive Web App gives you 90% of the native experience at 10% of the cost."**

- Installable from the browser (no App Store needed)
- Works offline
- Push notifications (on Android, coming to iOS)
- Loads instantly, even on slow connections
- No updates to manage — always the latest version

---

## Slide 3: What You Get

**"A fully branded, custom PWA — live in days, not months."**

- Your branding, your domain, your colors
- Mobile-first, responsive design
- Offline functionality built in
- SEO-friendly (indexable by search engines)
- Works on every device and browser
