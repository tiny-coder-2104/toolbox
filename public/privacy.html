Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-08. TinyCoder is a solo-operated project. This page states what is collected, in plain language.

Tool inputs stay in your browser

The 5 dev tools (JSON, Base64, Regex, URL, UUID) run 100% client-side. Pasted content never leaves your device. It is never sent to us, to the chat widget, or to any automation.

Chat is automated, and contact details are stored

The on-site chat assistant is automated (not a human). If you share your email, name, or company in chat, it is sent via an automation (Make.com webhook) to a private Google Sheet ("TinyCoder Leads") so the builder can follow up. Only messages containing a valid email address and a follow-up request are stored — see "Your choices" below. Test with throwaway addresses if you prefer.

Email follow-up

If you share your email, you may receive up to 4 follow-up emails over ~14 days. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe, honored within 10 business days. Free and paid downloads via Gumroad are handled by Gumroad's checkout — Gumroad's own privacy policy applies there.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity measure page views, tool usage counts, and chat-open events. No tool input content is sent to analytics.

Your choices

  • Don't share your email in chat — the tools work without it.
  • Unsubscribe from any follow-up email (one click).
  • Ask for deletion: paid buyers reply via their Gumroad purchase receipt email; everyone else can open an issue at tiny-coder-2104/toolbox.

Contact

Paid buyers: reply via your Gumroad purchase receipt email. Everyone else: GitHub issues at tiny-coder-2104/toolbox. No data is sold, ever.
